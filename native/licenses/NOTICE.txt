JPDFium Native Libraries - Third-Party Notices and Licenses
===========================================================

The platform-specific jpdfium-natives-* jars bundle the core PDF engine. The
optional jpdfium-natives-vips-* jars bundle libvips and its image codecs. A
jar contains only the notices for the libraries it actually ships; the full
license texts live beside this file.
