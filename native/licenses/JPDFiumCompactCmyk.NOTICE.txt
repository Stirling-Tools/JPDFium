JPDFiumCompactCmyk.icc

A generated, generic 4-component CMYK output profile for systems that have no
printing-condition ICC profile installed.

The profile is generated by native/tools/gen_compact_icc.c from the CC0
CGATS001Compat-v2-micro.icc profile by Clinton Ingram (saucecontrol):
https://github.com/saucecontrol/Compact-ICC-Profiles

CGATS001Compat-v2-micro.icc is distributed under CC0 and was produced with
LittleCMS/Argyll. The generator samples its CMYK -> Lab A2B0 table onto a small
A2B CLUT and inverts the same response to build the B2A CLUT with a strong
black-generation preference. No Ghostscript code, data, or ICC profile is used.

It is a generic fallback, not a certified characterization of a real press,
ink, or paper.
Use a vendor or standard profile (for example FOGRA, SWOP, or JapanColor) when
the output intent must identify a specific printing condition.

The generator keeps the profile compact by linking the perceptual and
colorimetric A2B/B2A tables, and it caps total area coverage at 245%. Neutral
colours use single-channel K ink.

JPDFium releases this file under the same MIT terms as the rest of the project.
